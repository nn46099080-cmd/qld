# QLD Alert Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

QLD Alert processes device notification tokens, app language, purchase
verification tokens, and limited technical request information needed to
provide alerts, verify purchases, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

The app uses Google Play Billing, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Mobile Ads,
Cloudflare, Finnhub, and Yahoo Finance market data. These providers may process
data under their own privacy policies.

Advertising is requested only after the applicable consent flow. Verified
paid-alert device registrations are retained while needed to deliver the
purchased service. Reinstalling the app or replacing a device registration
replaces the prior registration for that purchase.

Users can request deletion or ask privacy questions through the developer
contact email displayed on the QLD Alert Google Play listing.

QLD Alert is not directed to children.
